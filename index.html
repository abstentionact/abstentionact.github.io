01 OVERVIEW

Compliance bias.

We define compliance bias as the tendency to follow instructions even when the information, evidence, or authority needed to act safely is missing.

AbstentionAct tests whether agents recognise when information, evidence, or authority is missing. In those cases, they should ask for clarification, explain what remains uncertain, or seek an authorised handoff.

We also include safe tasks because agents should still complete requests when there is no reason to hold back.

02 THE BENCHMARK v0.16-rc1

Environments and scoring.

Tasks run in simulated HR and DevOps systems whose tools permit unsafe actions. A protected service log records what the agent actually does, while a separate verifier checks safety and completion. Two outcome tasks also use an LLM judge to check unsupported success claims.
